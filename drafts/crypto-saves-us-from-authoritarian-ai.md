# How Crypto Saves Us from Authoritarian AI

**Status:** Draft
**Notes:** Shortened version. Weave in subversion theme. Consider as follow-up to "10 years in crypto" piece.

---

The discourse around AI is exhausting. Doomers say we're dead. Accelerationists say paradise is 18 months away. Both miss the point.

The question isn't whether AI is good or bad. It's **who controls it**.

The most powerful technology ever created is being built by three companies, a handful of governments, and a few thousand people. No oversight. No distribution. No countervailing force.

History is clear: powerful technology gets captured by powerful entities. Every time. Unless something subverts it.

---

## The Default Future

If you do nothing, AI centralizes. Training costs favor scale. Data advantages compound. Regulatory moats get built. Three companies win.

In this future:

1. A few entities decide what AI can do
2. A few entities decide who gets access
3. A few entities capture the economic value
4. A few entities decide what's "true"

This isn't speculation. It's the base case.

---

## What Crypto Actually Is

Most people think crypto is digital money or a casino. It's been both.

But at its core, crypto is a 15-year project answering one question: **how do you build powerful systems that no single entity controls?**

Bitcoin didn't reform central banking. It subverted it. Ethereum didn't improve centralized computing. It routed around it. That's how crypto operates—not by asking permission, but by building alternatives that make permission irrelevant.

That subversive DNA is exactly what's needed now.

---

## The Convergence

The tools crypto built map directly onto AI's problems.

**AI can fake anything.** Crypto built cryptographic proof—digital signatures, timestamps, zero-knowledge proofs. Verification infrastructure for when truth becomes malleable.

**AI captures economic value.** Crypto built programmable ownership—tokens that distribute equity to participants, not just owners.

**AI agents need to transact.** Crypto built the rails—wallets, stablecoins, smart contracts. Banks won't serve bots. This infrastructure already exists.

**AI enables perfect surveillance.** Crypto built privacy tech—ZK proofs, encrypted computation, self-sovereign identity. Tools for a world where centralized systems can't be trusted with perfect information.

The industry was building for this moment. It just didn't know it.

---

## The Subversive Ingredient

Here's what polite AI discourse misses: you don't counter centralized power with approved tools.

Every other solution requires permission. Regulation requires governments to act against their interests. Corporate ethics requires companies to leave money on the table.

Crypto requires nothing. It just exists. Permissionless. Uncapturable. Subversive by design.

The same qualities that annoyed banks and regulators—the inability to control it, shut it down, co-opt it—those are the qualities that matter against AI centralization.

You can't fight capture with tools that can be captured.

## The Fork

Two AI futures:

**Authoritarian:** 3 companies control the models. Surveillance is total. Truth is whatever the platform says.

**Decentralized:** Open models anyone can run. Privacy by default. Truth is cryptographically verifiable.

The first is the default. The second requires building. The building blocks are crypto.

If you're in crypto, stop apologizing. The industry isn't building a casino. It's building the immune system for the most powerful technology of the century.

The AI future is either decentralized or authoritarian. You don't get the first one by asking nicely.

You get it by building something subversive enough to matter.
